Education Privacy Statement

Privacy Policy for Virtual Physics Lab

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

Package Name: com.cbse.virtualphysicslab

1. Overview

Virtual Physics Lab is an educational simulation tool designed for Senior Secondary students (Classes XI and XII) studying physics under the CBSE, NCERT, and State Board curricula. We are committed to protecting student and user privacy.

2. Zero Data Collection

Virtual Physics Lab does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, location data, device identifiers, or analytics. The application operates entirely offline on the user's device.

3. Permissions

The application requests only basic internet access permissions to render local web assets via the Android WebView component. It does not access contacts, camera, microphone, storage, or location services.

4. Children & Student Privacy (COPPA / FERPA Compliance)

Our application is intended for educational purposes and complies with student privacy standards. Because we do not collect any personal data whatsoever, no personal information from children or minors is ever solicited or retained.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The application contains NO advertisements, no third-party tracking SDKs, and no social media integrations.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via your designated educational institution or support contact email.
